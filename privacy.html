Privacy Policy – RTL Text Fixer

Last updated: October 23, 2026

RTL Text Fixer does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

  • Offline only. The app never connects to the internet. It has no analytics, telemetry, ads, crash reporting or third-party SDKs.
  • Text you convert. Text you type or paste into the window, or that is on the clipboard when you press the hotkey, is processed in memory on your device. When you use the hotkey the converted text is placed on the clipboard. Text is never saved to disk or sent anywhere.
  • Settings (options, hotkey, interface language) are stored in a local file on your PC and are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Children. The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Microsoft Store.
